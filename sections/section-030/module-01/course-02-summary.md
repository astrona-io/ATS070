# Summary

Production Backstage operation combines a controlled application artifact with runtime configuration, identity, database persistence and authorized integrations. Process readiness is narrower than functional feature health. Scaling and release decisions must account for stateful dependencies, migrations and scheduled work. A dependable deployment has explicit owners for credentials, backups, recovery and upgrades, rather than assuming that packaging the portal completes its operational design.
